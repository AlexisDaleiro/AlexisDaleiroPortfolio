# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated: Astro (static output) deployed on Vercel. Chosen because a portfolio is content-first, ships near-zero JS by default, and Astro has built-in i18n routing for the ES/EN requirement.

## Users

Recruiters and hiring teams at companies looking for a junior full-stack developer. Two markets at once:

- **International remote** companies, reading in English.
- **Local Uruguay** companies, reading in Spanish.

Their job: decide quickly whether Alexis is worth an interview, by checking skills, real code, and how to get in touch.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Alexis Daleiro, junior full-stack developer. It exists to get interviews. Success means a recruiter can, in a short visit, understand what Alexis builds, verify it in public code, and reach out or download a CV.

## Positioning

Junior full-stack generalist whose work is verifiable: every claim points to public GitHub code. No specialized niche has been confirmed beyond that.

## Operating Context

- Recruiters scan many candidates; first viewport and skimmability matter.
- Visitors often arrive from LinkedIn, job boards, or a CV link, frequently on mobile.
- Evaluation usually continues on GitHub, so project entries link to repos.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual ES/EN with a language toggle; both languages are first-class, not a machine-translated afterthought.
- Static site; no backend required.
- Open decisions: GitHub username/URL, contact channels (email, LinkedIn), whether a CV PDF is offered, own domain vs `*.vercel.app`, which repos are featured.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Alexis Daleiro. No other voice, logo, or identity commitments confirmed.

## Evidence on Hand

- Public GitHub repositories (specific repos not yet selected).
- No client testimonials, no published production projects, no professional work history confirmed. Do not fabricate testimonials, client logos, metrics, employers, or years of experience.

## Product Principles

1. Every claim is verifiable: link to code instead of asserting skill.
2. Recruiter time is scarce: who, what, proof, and contact must be reachable without hunting.
3. Both markets are equal: Spanish and English content stay in parity.
4. Honest junior framing: show growth and real work, never inflated seniority.
